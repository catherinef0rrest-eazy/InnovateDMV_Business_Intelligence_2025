## Overview

## Key Insights

1. **Solar Energy Dominance**: The only clean energy industry present in Virginia is solar electric power generation, concentrated in the Northern Virginia region.

2. **Regional Concentration**: All clean energy businesses are located in North Virginia (Fairfax and Loudoun counties), with no presence in Southwest Virginia.

3. **Industry Gaps**: There are no businesses in Virginia for:
   - Wind power generation
   - Battery manufacturing
   - Turbine manufacturing
   - Carbon capture chemical manufacturing

4. **Economic Impact**: The 6 solar businesses employ 17 people total and generate approximately $1.45 million in annual payroll.

5. **Geographic Distribution**: Clean energy businesses are exclusively located in the most populous and economically developed counties in Northern Virginia.

## Data Source
- **Dataset**: Census Bureau County Business Patterns (CBP) 2021
- **Analysis Date**: August 6, 2025
- **Total Counties Analyzed**: 36 Virginia counties and cities
- **API Source**: U.S. Census Bureau API

## Methodology
This analysis examined all business establishments in Virginia counties for the specified NAICS codes. The data includes:
- Number of establishments
- Total employment
- Annual payroll
- NAICS code and description

Counties were categorized into regions based on geographic location and economic characteristics.
